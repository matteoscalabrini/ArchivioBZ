# Defusing by form: references on changing the form of charged material

Reference chat pasted by the author on 2026-11-10, kept word for word below. It is where the
project's direction was worked out: charged material (fascist propaganda first, radical texts
more widely) can be defused by a change of form: typeface, wording, colour, scale, layout, voice.

The single words after each item (`arts`, `slwoods`, `wikipedia`…) are what remains of that
chat's citation chips: site names only, no addresses. Everything here is a lead until its source
has been read; the records in `files/01_references/` say, per reference, what a source confirmed.

Counter-references: Tommy Robinson's Comic Sans book cover, the Daily Stormer style guide,
Laibach / NSK over-identification, "Gay clown Putin", the debate around the annotated Mein Kampf.

**replacing fascist fonts in propaganda with funny fonts are there projects about this?**

I couldn’t find a well-known project that systematically resets fascist propaganda in Comic Sans or similar “silly” fonts. There are several close relatives, though.

Closest to your idea (typeface swaps)

“Hitler Archive: The Unpublished Design” (Rada Musikarat, UAL student project, 2023). She collected newspapers, posters and other Nazi propaganda material and changed all the blackletter into Futura, then printed them and presented them in an archive box. The swap is ironic rather than funny: it plays on Hitler’s own 1941 ban on Fraktur.
arts
Errol Morris’s Comic Sans experiment is the empirical basis for your idea. Compared with Baskerville, Helvetica, Georgia, Trebuchet and Computer Modern, Comic Sans made readers slightly less likely to believe a statement was true.
slwoods
One caveat cuts the other way: Tommy Robinson’s book cover set its title and author names in Comic Sans. The far right doesn’t always treat the font as a joke.
slwoods
Defusing fascist lettering in public space (Italy)

Bolzano, Casa del Fascio frieze (2017). Arnold Holzknecht and Michele Bernardi placed an illuminated Hannah Arendt quote, “No one has the right to obey”, in Italian, German and Ladin over the “Credere, obbedire, combattere” relief. Their minimal lettering was deliberately set against the grandiloquence of the fascist style.
wikipedia
inkl
“A + G” by Andrea Lo Giudice (Rome). He applies phosphorescent pigment to selected letters of fascist inscriptions on Rome’s buildings, so new phrases appear in the dark. The work degrades naturally over time.
shorttheatre
Archigraphiæ (ECAL + ISIA Urbino, 2018). This project mapped the geometric sans-serif lettering of Fascist Rome to take apart how it served propaganda. The book includes Jonathan Pierini’s essay “For the Liberation of Rationalist Lettering”. That makes it the natural reference if you want to digitize the original letterforms before swapping them.
ecal
hes-so
Humour as the weapon (symbols rather than fonts)

#PaintBack (Berlin, Ibo Omari). The group turns swastika graffiti into animals, flowers and other playful images, and publishes templates so anyone can do the same.
yahoo
laughingsquid
So the specific idea of fascist propaganda reset in comic fonts looks mostly unclaimed as a structured project. You’d mainly find scattered memes rather than a body of work.

**well also the communist manifesto in comic sans could be added**

Good catch, it fits as a precedent even though it’s not fascist material. The edition reprints the Manifesto, described on its back cover as one of history’s most influential texts, set in Comic Sans as “the most revolutionary font.” The joke goes further: the cover isn’t actually set in Comic Sans, and the front and back illustrations look like they were hand-drawn in Microsoft Paint, so the book reads less as a book turned into a meme and more as a meme that happens to be a book.
apod
apod

It shows the gag isn’t enough on its own, though. The Manifesto edition is self-ironic and affectionate rather than debunking. Swapping the type on fascist material would be read as ridicule because of the subject, not because of the font. That’s a different and sharper effect.

Source: The Communist Manifesto (In Comic Sans) – apod.li

**we need to go deeper, have more references. dont stop at fascism but also like the communist manifesto. defusing problematic ideas (or radical non necessarily problematic) via a change in font, text, colour, expression, size, etc.**

I’ll widen the search to font, text, colour, form, scale and voice interventions on charged texts and symbols, then group what I find by mechanism.

Here’s a wider set, grouped by what gets changed. The earlier references (Musikarat’s Futura swap, the Comic Sans Manifesto, Bolzano, #PaintBack) slot into these categories too.

Typeface and typographic container

Sans Bullshit Sans (Roel Nieskens, 2015). A font built on Droid Sans that uses only OpenType ligatures, no CSS or JavaScript, to replace listed buzzwords with a Comic Sans-styled censorship bar. This is the closest technical blueprint for your idea: the same trick with fascist slogans as the word list.
Sans Bullshit Sans: leveraging the synergy of ligatures
 +2
Hans Haacke, Der Bevölkerung (Reichstag, 2000). It reuses Peter Behrens’ lettering from “Dem Deutschen Volke” but swaps “Volk” for “Bevölkerung”, meaning everyone who lives in the country. The Bundestag approved it by 260 votes to 258. Same font, one word changed.
Wikipedia
wsws
Jenny Holzer, Inflammatory Essays (1979–82). The texts were reworked from Mao, Lenin, Goldman, Hitler and Trotsky. Each was forced into a fixed format: 100 words, 20 lines, italic capitals, coloured square paper, unsigned. The anonymity lets readers judge an ideology separately from the personality behind it.
artsy
massmoca
Il Male’s fake front pages (1978–82). Counterfeit editions of Paese Sera, La Stampa and Il Giorno announced Ugo Tognazzi’s arrest as head of the Red Brigades. A fake Repubblica ran “Lo Stato si è estinto” during Moro’s funeral. The typography of authority carries absurd content.
internazionale
Yes Men, NYT Special Edition (2008). A 14-page fake Times dated July 2009, with the motto bent into “All the news we hope to print”.
theyesmen
Swapping the text, keeping the form

Situationist détournement. Debord and Wolman’s 1956 manual is the theory behind most of this list. It warns that simple reversal is the most direct form and the least effective, and that the effect depends on the audience remembering the original.
theanarchistlibrary
Komar & Melamid, Sots Art. They stamped their own names under dry Soviet exhortations, turning them into ironic word art. Komar called it self-cleansing from the hypnosis of propaganda, and self-irony first of all.
artnet
theartstory
Exit-Deutschland’s “Trojan T-shirt” (2011). The shirts were donated to an NPD rock festival with a “Hardcore Rebels” print. After the first wash it vanished and revealed “If your T-shirt can do it, so can you”, plus Exit’s contact number. Here the text change is triggered by time.
exit-deutschland
adsoftheworld
Make America Kittens Again (2016). A browser extension that swaps photos of Trump for kittens. A companion extension replaced his name with “Someone With Tiny Hands”.
shortlist

Colour, costume, pose

Pink Tank, Prague (David Černý, 1991). He painted the Soviet tank monument pink and mounted a giant finger on it. The monument status was later abolished, and the tank was removed after repeated repaintings.

Soviet Army Monument, Sofia (2011). The collective Destructive Creation repainted the soldiers overnight as Superman, Joker, Robin, Captain America and others. The plinth got the caption “in step with the times”. It was later repainted pink for the Prague Spring anniversary and in Ukrainian colours.

Layout, format, apparatus

Mein Kampf, critical edition (IfZ Munich, 2016). The original text sits on the right-hand page, surrounded by more than 3,500 annotations on the left. The institute explicitly calls this framing a way of building a subtext and counter-text. The layout was designed by Gorbach Büro für Gestaltung. This is defusing by page design.
“Do you have a copy of the Bible?”: critical editions in the UL
 +2
Manga de Dokuha (East Press). The series published Das Kapital and Mein Kampf as manga. The Mein Kampf volume sold 45,000 copies and drew criticism from Bavaria. It’s a format change that arguably popularizes rather than defuses.

Serdar Somuncu reading Mein Kampf. Starting in 1996, he spent six years on stage, with over 1,500 performances for more than half a million people. Local officials in some cities tried to stop the shows.

Daily Stormer style guide (leaked 2017). It tells writers to keep the tone light because most people recoil from non-ironic hatred, and to leave readers unsure whether it’s a joke. Irony is also a far-right tool, which fits with the Tommy Robinson Comic Sans cover.

- **Francesco Arena, *Trittico 57*, Museion, 30 March to 6 May 2012** (row 12.34). Three travertine slabs, the same size and stone as the three added in 1957 to complete Hans Piffrader's frieze of Mussolini on horseback on the Casa Littoria (decided during a visit by President Gronchi). Arena sandblasted into them, in Morse code, the letter with which Italo Calvino left the Communist Party in *L'Unità* on 7 August 1957. [V] — [Museion](https://museion.it/en/exhibitions/75-trittico-57). It works on the same relief as the Arendt sentence of 2017 (rows 5.20 to 5.24) and five years earlier. A report in German says the sandblasting makes the letters hard to read. [SS] — [stol.it](https://www.stol.it/artikel/kultur/kunst/trittico-57-besiedelt-project-room-des-museion)
- **Gianni Pettena, *Secoli e millenni*, 2014** (row 12.35). A series of pencil drawings; the sheet held shows the Monument to Victory in ruins. Caption: "Gianni Pettena, from the series “Secoli e Millenni”, 2014. Courtesy of the artist and of Museion"; the image's alternative text is "Monument to Victory". [V] — [unibz](https://www.unibz.it/en/events/inhabited-dissonance-bozen-bolzano-1922-2). Shown at Kunst Meran in 2017 beside *Atta Unsar* (1973). [V] — [Kunst Meran](https://kunstmeranoarte.org/en/exhibitions/gianni-pettena-natural-architecture-en). Museion's catalogue record is said to describe the series as drawings of Bolzano in which architecture disappears into nature, with a sentence by Pettena that nature tells of millennia, not centuries. [SS] — the record's old address (kks_priref=30012094) now leads to the search form of the [Province's object catalogue](https://oggetti-musei-archivi.provincia.bz.it/search/simple); search "secoli e millenni" there by hand.
- **Rossella Biscotti, *Le teste in oggetto*, 2009** (row 12.33). "five bronze sculptures portraying the heads of King Victor Emmanuel III and Benito Mussolini", by Giovanni Prini and Domenico Rambelli for the cancelled Rome exhibition of 1942, found by Biscotti and Kevin van Braak in the storage rooms of the Palazzo degli Uffici at EUR and shown for a week at the Nomas Foundation, Rome; exhibition 4 April to 18 May 2009, curated by Maria Rosa Sossai. [V] — [Nomas Foundation](https://nomasfoundation.com/en/mostre/rossella-biscotti-le-teste-in-oggetto/). Museion holds *Le Teste in Oggetto*, 2015, silicone and acrylic resin on two Euro-pallets, each 100 × 118 × 50 cm. [V] — [Museion's record](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/le-teste-in-oggetto/7gFz_EQwXZHbsw). How the two relate is not stated. [INF]
- **DAAR (Sandi Hilal and Alessandro Petti), *Ente di Decolonizzazione – Borgo Rizza*, 2022** (row 12.36). A scaled reproduction of the façade of the main building of Borgo Rizza (Syracuse, 1940), divided into fifteen modules used for conversations in Naples, Berlin and Brussels and shown at the Biennale Architettura 2023; the aim is to "profane" such architecture, "to be used against themselves, and open for new common uses". [V] — [La Biennale](https://www.labiennale.org/en/architecture/2023/dangerous-liaisons/daar-alessandro-petti-and-sandi-hilal), [DAAR](https://www.decolonizing.ps/site/?p=5105). Golden Lion for the best participant, 20 May 2023. [V] — [La Biennale, awards](https://www.labiennale.org/en/news/awards-biennale-architettura-2023). The Sicilian colonisation agency that built the village is not in this research; the second report compares the Semirurali with the Pontine, Libyan, Ethiopian and Dodecanese settlements only.

**A lead that came with them.** The Pettena drawing was the image of "Inhabited Dissonance. Bozen-Bolzano 1922-2024", three evenings on 5 to 7 June 2024 at the Faculty of Design and Art of the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano, each pairing a historian with an artist. It belongs to the research project "Curating Bozen-Bolzano Fascist Legacy. A sustainable approach to a city's dissonant heritage" (CuFa), led by Roberto Gigliotti with Andrea Di Michele and Waltraud Kofler Engl, partners Lungomare and BAU, which announced an exhibition at the Galleria Civica for April 2025. [V] — [unibz](https://www.unibz.it/en/events/inhabited-dissonance-bozen-bolzano-1922-2). The same subject as this project, in the same city: its exhibition, its publications and its people are the next thing to look up.
