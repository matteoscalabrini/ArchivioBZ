# The bridge between Fascist "colonizzazione interna" and the Italianization of South Tyrol: offices, people, laws and words (1919-1943, scholarship to 2026)

Researcher notes, compiled 2026-10-26. Tags: [V] = I read it in the primary source (statute text, newsreel catalogue record, period document); [S] = read in a full secondary source (book excerpt, article, review); [SS] = search snippet or summary only; [INF] = my inference. Downloaded files are in `files/03_internal_colonization_bridge/`, listed in its `manifest.md`.

**Corrections to the brief, up front (each is sourced below):**
1. The CMCI was **not founded by a 1926 decree**. In 1926 the regime created a *Comitato permanente per le migrazioni interne* under the Ministry of Public Works. The *Commissariato per le migrazioni e la colonizzazione interna* dates from 1930, when Razza was appointed, and its organic law is **L. 9 April 1931 n. 358**.
2. The CMCI **did not end in 1939**. It is named in the July 1939 anti-urbanism law, and on 21 December 1939 it issued a circular on settlers for South Tyrol. From 1 March 1942 it was moved under the Ministry of Corporations "for the duration of the war". Its 1931 law was repealed only in 1961.
3. The ENTV did not begin in 1919. Its predecessor, the *Ente (di) Rinascita Agraria* (ERA), was set up as an ente morale by **R.D. 8 Sept 1921 n. 1343**; some scholars give 1920. It became the *Ente di rinascita agraria per le Tre Venezie* in 1931, gained wider expropriation powers by R.D.L. 7 Jan 1937 n. 82, and was renamed ENTV by **L. 27 Nov 1939 n. 1780**. That number is confirmed.
4. **Senate bill 328 (1949)** is real, but it concerns small peasant property. The ENTV appears in it only as administrator of optants' assets. It does carry useful figures.
5. The phrase **"manodopera regnicola e non allogena" is not in the text of R.D.L. 28 Sept 1934 n. 1621**; I read the full text. Secondary literature puts the "old-provinces workers" preference in the subsidy commission's practice. I could not verify the exact phrase anywhere.
6. I found no source for **"bonifica dei cognomi"**. The regime's own term was **"restituzione del cognome"**.

## Q1. The Commissariato per le Migrazioni e la Colonizzazione Interna (CMCI): founding, heads, remit, Agro Pontino, Sardinia, Bolzano, end, scholarship

### Takeaway
The CMCI was a Presidency-of-the-Council agency, created in 1930 around the agrarian syndicalist Luigi Razza and regulated by L. 358/1931. By law it controlled every inter-provincial transfer of workers and settler families "in tutto il Regno" and, with the Colonies ministry, "anche nelle Colonie". It chose the Veneto families sent to the Agro Pontino. Its own files explicitly cover Alto Adige: a CMCI circular of 21 Dec 1939 set the provinces from which settlers replacing departing South Tyroleans were to come. Its founder, as Minister of Public Works, proposed the 1935 decree that expropriated land for Bolzano's industrial zone.

### Cited Findings
- **Predecessor, 1926.** A *Comitato permanente per le migrazioni interne* was created in 1926 "emanazione del Ministero dei Lavori pubblici". It was later replaced by the CMCI "(legge 9 aprile 1931 n. 358), alle dirette dipendenze della Presidenza del Consiglio", with the task of directing transfers on the basis of "indagini preliminari di compensazione demografica" [S] — [Masi 2016, p. 37](https://www.icsaicstoria.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Masi_Bonifica_icsaic.pdf). I did not find the number of the 1926 decree.
- **Founding, 1930, and Razza.** "Nel 1930 Razza venne nominato commissario del neoistituito Commissariato per le migrazioni e la colonizzazione interna, della presidenza del Consiglio" [S, tertiary] — [it.wikipedia, Luigi Razza](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Razza). Gallo dates the body 1930-1940 and says it was "Creato grazie all'impulso del sindacalista agrario Luigi Razza" [V, publisher/ISUC text] — [ISUC press release, 9 Nov 2015](https://consiglio.regione.umbria.it/node/50226/printable/pdf).
- **L. 9 April 1931 n. 358, "Norme per la disciplina e lo sviluppo delle migrazioni e della colonizzazione interna"** (GU 27 Apr 1931 n. 96), signed "Mussolini" [V] — [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1931-04-09;358). Key articles [V]:
  - Art. 1: the CMCI "è posto alle dirette ed esclusive dipendenze del Capo del Governo".
  - Art. 2: its aim is "l'accertamento e alla razionale distribuzione della mano d'opera disponibile ... in tutto il Regno, e, di concerto con il Ministro per le colonie, anche nelle Colonie".
  - Art. 3: prefects send monthly reports including "il numero delle famiglie coloniche ... disposte a trasferirsi in altre Provincie".
  - Art. 6: the CMCI "a suo giudizio insindacabile" may order that part of the labour on any state-funded works "sia tratta da altre Provincie".
  - Art. 7: "Lo spostamento di gruppi di lavoratori e di famiglie coloniche da una Provincia per l'impiego in altra Provincia dovrà essere sempre disposto o autorizzato dal Commissariato".
  - Art. 12: it may encourage "le correnti migratorie verso le Colonie italiane per l'opera di colonizzazione di esse".
  - Art. 13: a "premio di colonizzazione" of up to L. 6,000 for families settled permanently "in zone di colonizzazione" since 28 Oct 1927.
- **Scenarios of activity.** "L'Agro pontino, la Libia, l'Africa Orientale, le città nuove della Sardegna, il quartiere romano dell'E42" [V, ISUC text] — [ISUC](https://consiglio.regione.umbria.it/node/50226/printable/pdf). Alto Adige and Bolzano are absent from this list.
- **Gallo's table of contents** [V] — [Gallo 2015 index, ISUC](https://consiglio.regione.umbria.it/sites/default/files/allegati_pubblicazioni/Indice_6.pdf). Stefano Gallo, *Il Commissariato per le migrazioni e la colonizzazione interna (1930-1940). Per una storia della politica migratoria del fascismo*, Foligno, Editoriale Umbra, 2015, 224 pp. [SS] — [UniPi profile](https://www.unipi.it/en/about-us/organisation/people/stefano-gallo-137387-en).
  - Introduction: "Le migrazioni interne e il regime" (p. 7).
  - Part I, "Il Commissariato per le migrazioni interne": "Sotto la protezione del duce" (p. 39); "Le ambizioni di Luigi Razza" (p. 50); "Le migrazioni interne al centro del ruralismo fascista" (p. 59).
  - Part II, "Bonifiche e popolamento" (p. 69): "La disciplina della mobilità territoriale: nascita del Cmci" (p. 69); "L'Agro pontino" (p. 89); "La Libia" (p. 106); "Altre colonizzazioni dirette, in Sardegna e nella costa dalmata" (p. 124).
  - Part III, "Emigrare da fascisti: l'Impero del lavoro" (p. 141): "Popolare l'impero: le operazioni dei Ventimila" (p. 167); "Progetti imperiali: Carbonia e l'E42" (p. 178); "Collocamento e mobilità alla fine degli anni Trenta" (p. 192).
  - **No chapter on Alto Adige.**
- **Reviews of Gallo.** The book was presented in Naples (30 June 2015; Michele Colucci, Gian Carlo Falco) and in Perugia (11 Nov 2015; Colucci, Alessio Gagliardi) [V] — [Naples flyer](https://consiglio.regione.umbria.it/sites/default/files/allegati_eventiagenda/30-06%20Presentazione%20volume%20Gallo_Napoli.pdf); [ISUC](https://consiglio.regione.umbria.it/node/50226/printable/pdf). I found no review.
- **Annual reports (period primary sources)**, as listed by Masi [S] — [Masi fn. 3](https://www.icsaicstoria.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Masi_Bonifica_icsaic.pdf):
  - PCM, Comitato, *Le migrazioni interne in Italia* (1928), and the same for 1929.
  - PCM, Commissariato, *Le migrazioni interne in Italia nell'anno 1930 ... 1934* (Rome 1931-35).
  - *Le migrazioni nel Regno e nelle Colonie nell'anno 1935* (1936).
  - *Le migrazioni nel Regno e nell'Africa Italiana*, Anni 1936-37, 1937-38 and 1938-39 (Rome 1937-39).
  - The change of title in 1935-36 shows the remit moving from internal to imperial [INF].
- **The CMCI chose the Pontine settlers.** "La scelta delle famiglie venete per colonizzare l'Agro Pontino, è fatta esclusivamente da un ente governativo, il C.M.C.I." The first 466 families (4,910 people) were "scelte su un totale di 1.820 famiglie con quasi 20.000 componenti" [S] — [Gaspari 1985, pp. 70-71 and ch. 3](https://famigliaperuzzo.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/oscar_gaspari_lemigrazione_veneta_nellag.pdf) (Oscar Gaspari, *L'emigrazione veneta nell'Agro Pontino durante il periodo fascista*, Brescia, Morcelliana 1985; poor-quality OCR scan).
- **CMCI and Alto Adige: the key document.** A circular of the CMCI to the PNF federal secretaries, dated **21 December 1939**, stated "that settlers destined to replace the South Tyroleans who would leave for Germany should come from the 56 northern and central provinces, north of Perugia". Location: ACS, Ministero del Lavoro e della Previdenza sociale, *Commissariato generale per le migrazioni e la colonizzazione interna*, b. 40G, fasc. "Circolari in partenza 1939". Southerners were "formally excluded from the Italianization of South Tyrol, both in public employment and in the plans for colonization and the replacement of the South Tyroleans" [S] — [Di Michele, JMIS 2023, pp. 106-107 and n. 22](https://doi.org/10.1080/1354571X.2022.2122369) (open-access PDF in files). Di Michele also cites his *L'italianizzazione imperfetta* (2003), pp. 227-228.
- **Pergher on the CMCI.** Her index lists "Commission for Migration and Internal Colonization (CMCI)" at pp. 77, 85, 101, 108, 122-124, 145, 225, 227 and 230, and "Migration and Colonization Commission (CMCI)" at pp. 79-80. Pages 225-230 sit in her chapter 5 on the 1939 Option [V, index only] — [Pergher index](https://assets.cambridge.org/97811084/19741/index/9781108419741_index.pdf). So Pergher uses CMCI material for South Tyrol after the Option. I could not read those pages.
- **The CMCI still existed in July 1939.** L. 6 July 1939 n. 1092 makes the CMCI the authority for workers moving between provinces (art. 2). Art. 3: "In caso di necessità, il Commissariato per le migrazioni e la colonizzazione può autorizzare lo spostamento temporaneo di gruppi di lavoratori da una Provincia ad un'altra" [V] — [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1939-07-06;1092).
- **1942 transfer.** R.D.L. 27 Feb 1942 n. 316 (converted by L. 13 June 1942 n. 858) provided: "Con decorrenza dal 1° marzo 1942-XX e per la durata dell'attuale guerra, il Commissariato per le migrazioni e la colonizzazione interna è posto alle dipendenze del Ministero delle corporazioni" [V] — [Normattiva, art. 1 as in force 2 May 1942](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1931-04-09;358~art1!vig=1942-06-01).
- **1961 repeal.** L. 358/1931 was repealed by L. 10 Feb 1961 n. 5, together with L. 1092/1939 [V, amendment list] — [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/do/atto/vediAggiornamentiAllAtto?atto.dataPubblicazioneGazzetta=1931-04-27&atto.codiceRedazionale=031U0358). The 1958 repeal bill by Terracini and others called the two laws contrary to art. 16 of the Constitution [V] — [Senate bill 143/1958](https://www.parlamento.it/service/PDF/PDFServer/DF/327251.pdf).
- **The ACS fonds.** It is catalogued as "Commissariato generale per le migrazioni e la colonizzazione interna" under the Ministero del Lavoro e della Previdenza sociale [S] — [Di Michele JMIS n. 22](https://doi.org/10.1080/1354571X.2022.2122369). This fits the 1942 move [INF].
- **Heads.**
  - Luigi Razza was commissioner from 1930. Biography [S, tertiary] — [it.wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Razza):
    - in 1919-20, after the war, editor of *Il Popolo* in Trento and secretary of the **Fascio di Trento** (1920);
    - Minister of Public Works from 24 Jan 1935;
    - died 7 Aug 1935 in an air crash near Cairo, en route to Eritrea.
  - Razza's successor at the CMCI is **not identified**. The searches were inconclusive. Giuseppe Lombrassa was Under-Secretary for Corporations from 26 Feb 1942 to 2 June 1943, which overlaps with the CMCI's move under that ministry. Nothing I read names him CMCI head [S] — [it.wikipedia, Lombrassa](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Lombrassa).
- **Razza and Bolzano: the direct link.** R.D.L. **7 March 1935 n. 234**, "Dichiarazione di pubblica utilità dei lavori di impianto e d'esercizio della zona industriale di Bolzano", was issued "Sulla proposta del Nostro Ministro Segretario di Stato per i lavori pubblici". It is countersigned "Mussolini - **Razza** - Di Revel" [V] — [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:regio.decreto.legge:1935-03-07;234). Its conversion law, L. 3 June 1935 n. 1093, is also signed "Mussolini - Razza - Di Revel" [V] — [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/eli/id/1935/07/04/035U1093/CONSOLIDATED/20091216). For the terms of the expropriation see Q5.

### Inferences
- [INF] The CMCI is the one office in which all three theatres meet: its law speaks of the "Regno" and the "Colonie"; it selected the Pontine Veneto families; it ran the ventimila; and in December 1939 it set the geography of settler recruitment for South Tyrol. That last point is the strongest documented institutional bridge to Bolzano.
- [INF] The person-level bridge is Razza himself. He was a 1920 Trento Fascio leader, the CMCI founder (1930-35) and, as Public Works minister, the proposer of the 1935 Bolzano industrial-zone expropriation decree. Gallo has a section titled "Le ambizioni di Luigi Razza" (p. 50).
- [INF] Gallo's TOC suggests the CMCI's Alto Adige role was minor or late, after the Option. The industrial workers who went to Bolzano in 1935-39 may have moved under the CMCI's general authorization powers (arts. 6-7 of L. 358/1931) rather than through a dedicated programme. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- Number and date of the 1926 Comitato decree and of the 1930 act founding the Commissariato. Gallo pp. 21-39 and 69ff. would settle this; the book is not online.
- Whether the CMCI annual reports for 1935-39 count transfers to the province of Bolzano. The volumes are not digitized as far as I found. Check them at the BNCR/ACS or the ISTAT library.
- CMCI head after Razza's death (Aug 1935).
- Whether Gallo's pp. 192-210 ("Collocamento e mobilità alla fine degli anni Trenta") discuss Bolzano.
- The UCL paper on coerced migration to the Pontine Marshes uses CMCI yearly reports 1930-38. It was blocked by Cloudflare; open it by hand: https://discovery-pp.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/13651/1/13651.pdf

## Q2. The Opera Nazionale Combattenti and the Agro Pontino: towns, podere types, settlers' origins, selection rules, architects, and the language of "bonifica integrale" / "bonifica umana"

### Takeaway
On 2,953 ONC poderi settled between 1932 and 1939, about 59% of the families came from the Veneto, Gaspari's count including Friuli (Udine). Selection, done by the CMCI, required ex-combatants, large families, and families "di pura razza italiana" from malarial, high-pressure districts. The ONC is also a direct Alto Adige actor: it drew up a 1926-27 plan to expropriate South Tyrolean land, ran farms there, and the ACS holds an ONC series titled "Alto Adige, Coloni". No Pontine architect is documented working in Bolzano before 1943. Marcello Piacentini is the architectural bridge: he approved the Littoria plan and designed Bolzano's Victory Monument and the 1934 city plan.

### Cited Findings
- **ONC founding.** It was created by decreto luogotenenziale of 10 Dec 1917 to assist war veterans [S] — [Archivio di Stato di Latina, preface](https://archiviodistatolatina.cultura.gov.it/fileadmin/risorse/foto_pagine/PDF/prefazione_GC_Comuni.pdf). In 1931 Mussolini entrusted the Pontine project to the ONC. Under the ONC, work went on all year round, with unknown deaths [SS, tertiary] — [it.wikipedia, Agro Pontino](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agro_Pontino). ONC commissioner: Valentino Orsolini Cencelli [SS] — [same](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agro_Pontino).
- **Towns: founding / inauguration** [S] — [AS Latina](https://archiviodistatolatina.cultura.gov.it/fileadmin/risorse/foto_pagine/PDF/prefazione_GC_Comuni.pdf):
  - Littoria: 30 June 1932 / 18 Dec 1932
  - Sabaudia: 5 Aug 1933 / 15 Apr 1934
  - Pontinia: 19 Dec 1934 / 18 Dec 1935
  - Aprilia: 25 Apr 1936 / 29 Oct 1937
  - Pomezia: 22 Apr 1938 / 29 Oct 1939
  - The province of Littoria was created on 18 Dec 1934 [S].
- **Scale by 1939.** About 65,500 ha reclaimed and 3,851 case coloniche: 2,953 built by the ONC and 898 by the Università Agrarie and private owners. Five towns and 17 borghi [S] — [Gaspari 1985, p. 70](https://famigliaperuzzo.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/oscar_gaspari_lemigrazione_veneta_nellag.pdf). Pergher gives "5 towns, 18 villages, and 3,000 farmsteads, settling them with families mostly from northern Italy" (1932-39) [V, excerpt p. 8] — [Pergher excerpt](https://assets.cambridge.org/97811084/19741/excerpt/9781108419741_excerpt.pdf). Dalle Mulle and Ambrosino, citing Linoli, give 2,953 farmhouses and 64,666 ha of parcels, and "the transfer of around 29,000 people between 1932 and 1939" [S] — [Hist. J. 2023, p. 899](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0018246X23000158).
- **ONC poderi built and colonist population by year** [S, OCR table] — [Gaspari p. 73](https://famigliaperuzzo.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/oscar_gaspari_lemigrazione_veneta_nellag.pdf):

- **Origins of ONC settler families (total 2,953).** "La stragrande maggioranza delle famiglie coloniche è originaria del Veneto (1748 famiglie pari al 59,2% del totale), al secondo posto ... Emilia-Romagna (549 famiglie pari al 18,6%), al terzo quelle del Lazio (solo le prov. di Roma e Littoria) con 366 famiglie, pari al 12,4%". The most numerous Veneto provinces were "Treviso, Udine, Padova, Rovigo, le province più povere del Veneto" [S] — [Gaspari pp. 71-72](https://famigliaperuzzo.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/oscar_gaspari_lemigrazione_veneta_nellag.pdf).
  - **Gaspari counts Friuli (Udine) inside "Veneto"**, as in the old Regno compartimento [S].
  - Per-province figures, from a garbled OCR table that should be checked against the book: Ferrara 412 (13.9%), Treviso 340 (11.5%), **Udine 308 (10.5%)**, Padova 276 (9.3%), Littoria 291 (10.0%), Rovigo 233 (7.9%), Vicenza 228 (7.7%), Verona 220 (7.4%), Venezia 114 (3.8%), Forlì 80 (2.7%), Roma 75 (2.5%), Belluno 22 (0.7%) [S, OCR].
  - "solo 291 famiglie coloniche dell'O.N.C., il 10% del totale, sono originarie della provincia di Littoria" [S].
  - Pontinia had 234 Littoria-province families (51.9%) and 160 Ferrarese (35.5%). Pomezia had only Forlivesi (80 families). Aprilia had 98 Veneto families out of 138 [S].
- **Other settlers, 1939-40.** Late arrivals at Aprilia and Pomezia included descendants of **Trentino** peasants who had emigrated c. 1880 to Mahovljani (Bosnia), Rovigo-origin Italians from Cataloi (Dobruja, Romania), and Veneto emigrants returning from France [S] — [Gaspari p. 73](https://famigliaperuzzo.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/oscar_gaspari_lemigrazione_veneta_nellag.pdf). The settler communities are known as "venetopontine"; Friulian and Ferrarese-Emilian groups were also present [SS, tertiary] — [it.wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comunit%C3%A0_venetopontine). One municipal page gives "about 60,000 farmers from the Veneto, Friuli and Emilia" from Oct-Nov 1932 [SS]. That figure conflicts with Gaspari's 29,300 colonists by 1939 and probably includes workers [INF] — [Comune di Latina](https://www.comune.latina.it/bonifica-integrale-english-version/).
- **Selection rules: the CMCI criteria**, quoted by Gaspari from Salvatore Collari, *La redenzione dell'Agro Pontino* (period publication, issued "a cura del Ministero ...") [S] — [Gaspari pp. 70-71](https://famigliaperuzzo.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/oscar_gaspari_lemigrazione_veneta_nellag.pdf):
  - Settlement is "lo scopo supremo" of bonifica integrale: "è il potenziamento demografico che domina sul rendimento agricolo".
  - "I nuovi coloni dovevano essere anzitutto rurali 'fedeli alla terra' ... un'entità omogenea, compatta nella sua solida composizione famigliare, unita tutta attorno al suo Capo; dovevano dare assoluta garanzia di sanità fisica come di rettitudine morale, di fede politica".
  - They had to come "da zone a forte pressione demografica", be ex-combatants, be "di pura razza italiana", and come from a malarial zone.
  - "Si vide così, che la regione che più di tutte si presentava conforme ... era quella veneta, le cui caratteristiche etniche, economiche e sociali, si armonizzavano colle prestabilite qualità politiche, demografiche e sanitarie".
  - Gaspari's reading: private landowners preferred Marchigiani sharecroppers, while the CMCI chose Veneti, which shows "la natura politica della colonizzazione" [S].
- **Tenure and the podere.** Each family got a podere with a casa colonica (often two floors, kitchen and store on the ground floor), a stable, livestock and tools. It took on a "debito colonico" to be repaid in produce, with "riscatto" at the end [SS] — [it.wikipedia Agro Pontino](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agro_Pontino); [AS Latina](https://archiviodistatolatina.cultura.gov.it/fileadmin/risorse/foto_pagine/PDF/prefazione_GC_Comuni.pdf). Average podere of about 20 ha, varying with soil [S, AS Latina]. "Quasi l'84% dei coloni firma un semplice contratto d'affitto". In 1939 only 400 of 2,953 poderi were self-sufficient (13.5%) [S, Gaspari, OCR]. Of 1,998 families present at one date, 77 (3.8%) had been expelled by the ONC [S, Gaspari].
- **Architects** [S, tertiary]:
  - **Littoria**: plan by Oriolo Frezzotti "con l'approvazione di Marcello Piacentini" — [it.wikipedia Latina](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latina).
  - **Sabaudia**: the ONC competition of 21 Apr 1933 was won by Piccinato with Cancellotti, Montuori and Scalpelli — [it.wikipedia Sabaudia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabaudia).
  - **Pontinia**: engineer Alfredo Pappalardo with Frezzotti as artistic consultant — [it.wikipedia Pontinia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontinia).
  - **Aprilia, Pomezia and Fertilia** (Sardinia): Concezio Petrucci's group, which also did Segezia (Foggia, 1938-42) — [it.wikipedia Petrucci](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concezio_Petrucci).
- **Did any of them work in Bolzano or the colonies?** [S, tertiary]:
  - **Montuori** worked on Carbonia, a CMCI scenario, and on the mining town of Arsia in **Istria**, a "new province" — [it.wikipedia Montuori](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenio_Montuori).
  - **Piccinato** made Bolzano's *Piano regolatore generale* in **1964**, after the war — [it.wikipedia Piccinato](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Piccinato).
  - **Frezzotti** built the Galleria Tirrena in **Trento** in 1949-52 — [it.wikipedia Frezzotti](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oriolo_Frezzotti).
  - For **1919-43** I found no Bolzano work by Frezzotti, Piccinato, Cancellotti, Montuori, Scalpelli or Petrucci.
  - **Marcello Piacentini** is the bridge figure. He approved the Littoria plan; designed the Bolzano Victory Monument; received the 1934 Bolzano city-plan commission; and sat with prefect Mastromattei on the jury of the Aug 1934 competition "La casa per l'Alto Adige", which was meant to replace Tyrolean-style farmhouses with Italian family houses [S] — [Dunajtschik/Steinacher, GR/SR 17 (2008), pp. 116-117, 121-123](https://geschichteundregion.eu/attachment/get/up_90_16520957026012.pdf).
- **ONC in Alto Adige.**
  - In 1926-27 the regime, with the ONC, planned to expropriate 1,000 ha from German-speaking tenants and settle 200-600 families from the old provinces between Merano and Lavis. "Lack of resources, foreign policy concerns, and the opposition of the Tyrolean tenants derailed the project" [S] — [Dalle Mulle & Ambrosino 2023, p. 897](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0018246X23000158), citing Di Michele, "Fascismo, bonifica e politiche d'insediamento in Alto Adige negli anni '20", *Passato e presente* 113 (2021) 64-82, doi:10.3280/PASS2021-113005 [SS abstract] — [FrancoAngeli](https://www.francoangeli.it/riviste/articolo/68456).
  - ACS holds an ONC series "Servizio Agrario, Alto Adige, Coloni, box 10". ### Inferences
- [INF] The Pontine selection criteria (rural, ex-combatant, large family, "pura razza italiana", politically reliable, northern) are the template the CMCI applied again in 1939 to South Tyrol: settlers only from the 56 provinces north of Perugia, southerners excluded. Both are positive selection of a "loyal" settler body, a recognisable settler-colonial feature.
- [INF] Udine/Friuli at about 10.5% plus the other Veneto provinces at about 48.6% means the north-east supplied the majority of Pontine families. The same north-east (Veneto, Trentino, Friuli) is named as the main source of Bolzano's industrial immigrants (Q5). That points to a shared recruitment basin rather than a shared institution.

## Q3. The Ente Nazionale per le Tre Venezie (ENTV): origins, 1930s revival, the 1939 law, the Option, 1949 Senate bill 328, holdings by 1943, and Di Michele's *Terra italiana*

### Takeaway
The ENTV was the land-acquisition arm of South Tyrol's "conquista del suolo". It started as a Veneto war-reconstruction body (ERA, 1920/21). It was redirected in 1931 to buy land from allogeni in the new provinces, given wider expropriation powers in 1937, and in 1939 renamed and charged, in coded statutory language, with taking over and reselling the optants' property. Before the Option it had moved only 325 farms and 8,933 ha (0.6% of farmland in the new provinces) to Italian hands. In 1949 it still administered 52 farm units of 12-20 ha in the Val d'Adige, leased to settlers "from various zones" who had been promised ownership.

### Cited Findings
- **Origins.**
  - L. 1780/1939 art. 1 says the "Ente di rinascita agraria per le Tre Venezie" was "eretto in ente morale col R. decreto 8 settembre 1921, n. 1343" [V] — [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1939-11-27;1780).
  - Di Michele describes the ERA as founded in 1921 "con finalità di razionalizzazione dell'attività produttiva nelle campagne venete" [V, project text] — [unibz](https://www.unibz.it/assets/Documents/Competence-Centres/unibz-storiaregionale-DiMichele-Conquista-del-suolo.pdf).
  - Dalle Mulle and Ambrosino date it to 1920; it "mainly administered war-damaged farmlands in Veneto" [S] — [Hist. J. p. 898](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0018246X23000158).
  - Pergher also lists a predecessor, the "Agency for the Reconstruction and Agrarian Rebirth of the Provinces of Venice and Treviso" (p. 101) [V, index].
- **1931 redirection.** In Aug 1931 the government "transformed it into the Ente di Rinascita Agraria delle Tre Venezie ... and gave it the specific purpose of buying land from owners belonging to the minority populations of South Tyrol and Venezia Giulia". It bought about 3,000 ha in Gorizia before 1933, then refocused on Bolzano [S] — [Hist. J. p. 898](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0018246X23000158). The statute was R.D. 14 Aug 1931 n. 1188, amended by R.D. 21 July 1933 n. 1146 and R.D. 28 June 1938 n. 1326 [V, repealed by art. 22 of L. 1780/1939].
- **1937.** "Il regio decreto legge 7 gennaio 1937, n. 82 ampliava i poteri di esproprio dell'ERA, mentre altri provvedimenti ne indirizzavano l'attività specificatamente nelle aree di confine" [V, project text] — [Di Michele, unibz](https://www.unibz.it/assets/Documents/Competence-Centres/unibz-storiaregionale-DiMichele-Conquista-del-suolo.pdf). R.D.L. 82/1937 was converted by L. 13 Dec 1937 n. 2368 [V, L. 1780 art. 22].
- **Results before the Option.** "As of 30 September 1938, 325 farms, accounting for 8,933 hectares of land, had passed from the property of individuals identified as allogeni to Italian hands through the Ente's purchases. That represented only 0.6 per cent of the productive agricultural surface of the new provinces" [S] — [Hist. J. p. 898, citing "Appunto per il Duce", 18 Oct 1938, ACS PCM 1940-43 fo. 3/1-1 n. 8246](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0018246X23000158). A blog by the same research group says these went to 245 families [SS] — [themythofhomogeneity.org, Feb 2019](https://themythofhomogeneity.org/2019/02/).
- **Obstacles.** Tyrolean farms were intensive and expensive and needed skilled labour hard to find in the old provinces. There was competition from minority buyers backed by German or Austrian capital. Italian sharecroppers fell into debt and "shrewdly described their holdings as bastions of the nation in hostile territory" to ask for subsidies [S] — [Hist. J. pp. 898-899](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0018246X23000158).
- **L. 27 Nov 1939 n. 1780**, "Trasformazione dell'Ente di rinascita agraria per le Tre Venezie in «Ente nazionale per le Tre Venezie»" (GU 12 Dec 1939 n. 287). Signed by Mussolini, PNF secretary Muti, Finance minister Di Revel, Agriculture minister Tassinari, Corporations minister Ricci, Popular Culture minister Pavolini and one more minister (see the Normattiva text for the exact countersignatures); seen by Guardasigilli Grandi [V] — [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1939-11-27;1780); [GU pdf](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/1939/12/12/287/sg/pdf). Key provisions [V]:
  - Art. 1: extends its action "ad ogni forma di attività economica sociale ed educativa". Seat in Rome "presso la Presidenza del Consiglio dei Ministri". Its jurisdiction includes the province of **Zara**.
  - Art. 2: it may carry out "opere di bonifica, trasformazione e riordinamento della proprietà fondiaria", buy urban property, and take over and transfer industrial, commercial and hotel businesses.
  - Art. 3: it "può chiedere il trasferimento in sua proprietà di immobili, a chiunque appartengano".
  - Art. 9: it is run by a *Commissario del Governo*, advised by a committee that includes a member of the PNF national directorate.
  - Art. 14(b): finance through IMI "per conto e nell'interesse dello Stato", including "rimborso di differenze di cambio negli eventuali trasferimenti all'estero del valore dei beni rilevati".
  - Art. 21: tax penalties may be waived on operations "con trasferimento all'estero del valore di beni, di aziende ... o con trasferimento all'estero di esercizi professionali".

- **The 1939 mandate.** L. 1780/1939 assigned the ENTV "il delicatissimo e impegnativo compito di prendere in consegna i beni immobili degli optanti per poi rivenderli attraverso aste o trattative private. Nel giro di un biennio l'ENTV entrò in possesso di una considerevole quantità di beni, tra cui masi e terreni agricoli, ma anche case, appartamenti, alberghi, esercizi commerciali e studi professionali" [V, project text] — [Di Michele, unibz](https://www.unibz.it/assets/Documents/Competence-Centres/unibz-storiaregionale-DiMichele-Conquista-del-suolo.pdf). - **Settlement planning, 1940-41.** Plans were drawn up only between late 1940 and early 1941, after the Option had closed. Source: ENTV, "Modalità di cessione dei beni rustici situati nel territorio degli accordi italo-germanici", Jan 1941, ACS PCM 1940-43 fo. 3/1-1 n. 8246/6. *Il Sole 24 Ore* ("Attività da riordinare", 4 Nov 1939) had urged the government to plan [S] — [Hist. - **Senate bill 328 (1948-49 session)**, by senators Piemonte, Salomone and Carelli, communicated 26 March 1949: "Norme aggiuntive al decreto legislativo 24 febbraio 1948, n. 114, recante provvidenze a favore della piccola proprietà contadina". ### Inferences
- [INF] Statutory euphemism is itself evidence. The 1939 law hides the optants behind "trasferimenti all'estero del valore dei beni" and gives a Rome-based government commissioner power to take property "a chiunque appartengano". This is the legal form of land transfer from one ethnic group to state-selected settlers.

## Q4. Roberta Pergher, *Mussolini's Nation-Empire* (CUP 2017/2018): argument, structure, quotations, reviews; other scholarship using "settler colonialism" or "internal colonialism" for South Tyrol, and the counter-argument

### Takeaway
Pergher argues that in South Tyrol and Libya, Fascist population settlement was a sovereignty strategy, not a cure for overpopulation: a way to "secure and legitimize rule over contested lands". She explicitly engages settler-colonial theory (Wolfe, Veracini) and "internal colonialism". The strongest counter-reading is Dalle Mulle and Ambrosino (2023) and Di Michele (2023). For them, Fascist policy aimed at assimilation, with distrust. Settlement was a catalyst for absorption ("beguiling encirclement"), not replacement, and South Tyroleans were idealised as model peasants, not treated as colonial subjects. Both camps nonetheless call the land policy "colonization".

Steinacher co-authored the "Ethnozid/Landnahme" reading [S, above]. Steininger, *Südtirol im 20. Jahrhundert*, pp. 109-115, is the source for the industrial-zone immigration mechanisms [S, cited by Dunajtschik/Steinacher]. Carlo Romeo writes on the Italian population 1943-45 [V title only] — [GR/SR](https://geschichteundregion.eu/attachment/get/up_154_16232251572713.pdf).

### Inferences
- [INF] For the studio's thesis, the defensible formulation is that the policy was settler-colonial in **means** (state-selected settlers, land transfer, expropriation on punitive terms, new settlements, recruitment basins that excluded both natives and southerners) while its declared **end** was assimilation, not elimination. That is close to Pergher's "nation-empire" and Veracini's distinction. Dalle Mulle, Ambrosino and Di Michele attack the "replacement/racial subject" reading, not the "colonization" label: Di Michele's own project calls it "politiche italiane di colonizzazione dell'Alto Adige".
- [INF] Wolfe's "logic of elimination" fits best the 1939 Option moment: Tolomei's "La trasmigrazione" and the CMCI's December 1939 replacement circular (Q5, Q1). It fits the 1923-38 assimilationist phase less well.

### Gaps
- Pergher pp. 97-116 and 164-165, where she engages settler colonialism, Wolfe and Veracini directly: no preview access (Google Books API quota blocked). The page numbers are verified from the index; the arguments on those pages are not.
- Full texts of the Ballinger, Agnew, Ebner and Baxa reviews (paywalled or bot-blocked).
- Fuller 2019 chapter text; Lehmann 2000 dissertation.

## Q5. Vocabulary, with dated examples: allogeni, regnicoli, bonifica, colonizzazione, popolamento, "sfollare le città", "quarta sponda", "terre redente"

### Takeaway
Several terms are verified in statute or period text:
- "allogeni" in a 1926 decree-law title and in 1927 Gerarchia articles;
- "colonizzazione interna" in the CMCI's legal name and law;
- "famiglie coloniche", "zone di colonizzazione" and "premio di colonizzazione" (1931);
- "urbanesimo" (1928, 1939);
- "bonifica" in the ENTV law;
- the ONC's archival label "Alto Adige, Coloni".
"Restituzione del cognome" (not "bonifica dei cognomi") was the surname term. "Regnicoli" and the 1934 "manodopera regnicola e non allogena" phrase remain unverified.

### Inferences
- [INF] The regime's lexicon has two registers. In the legal/technical register (colonizzazione interna, famiglie coloniche, bonifica, trasformazione fondiaria, pubblica utilità), Alto Adige operations are named with the same words as the Agro Pontino. In the national register (redenzione, restituzione, allogeni and alloglotti, "rediscover themselves"), the locals are framed as lapsed Italians to be recovered. That second register is what distinguishes South Tyrol from Libya, where the natives were "sudditi" (see Pergher ch. 4).
- [INF] "Allogeni" is the hinge word. It names the local population as other ("of another stock") without naming them German, and so allows both colonization (settlers) and assimilation (restitution of names) to be described as one policy.

### Gaps
- Earliest use of "allogeni" for South Tyroleans; Tolomei pre-1915 usage not checked.
- Source and exact wording of "manodopera regnicola e non allogena". Look for the ministerial decree setting contribution rules under art. 7 of R.D.L. 1621/1934 (1935?), or Lehmann 2000 pp. 145-146 and Steininger pp. 114-115.
- "Regnicoli" in South Tyrol: no verified period example.
- Italian originals of Tolomei 1927 and 1938, and of Colocci Vespucci 1919. Seen only in English translation.

## Q6. Regime publications, speeches and Luce newsreels that put Alto Adige and the colonies in one frame

### Takeaway

## Q8. Synthesis: the documented chain of offices, people, laws and words (for the report writer)

### Takeaway
A chain from the domestic colonization programme to the colonies to Bolzano can be documented at the level of laws ([V]), offices and people ([V]/[S]). It is weaker at the level of explicit regime rhetoric pairing Alto Adige with Africa: Tolomei 1938 is the main text. The chain runs ONC (1917) → ERA (1921) → CMCI (1930/31) → Pontine settlement (1932-39) → ERATV purchases in Alto Adige (1931-38) → Bolzano industrial zone (1934-36) → Libyan ventimila (1938) → anti-urbanism law (1939) → Option and ENTV (1939) → CMCI replacement circular (Dec 1939) → ENTV holdings (1949).

### Inferences
- [INF] The bridge is stronger in personnel and statute than in rhetoric. The same men (Razza, Cobolli Gigli, Mastromattei, Volpi, Piacentini, Tolomei) and the same instruments (expropriation for "pubblica utilità", selected settler families, debt-to-ownership tenure, inter-provincial labour control by the CMCI) recur across the Agro Pontino, Libya and Bolzano.
- [INF] The counter-argument should be presented honestly. Pre-1939 land colonization in South Tyrol was small (0.6% of farmland). The urban-industrial route (Bolzano zone, workers' quarters) did most of the demographic work. The declared goal remained assimilation, not removal, until the Option briefly opened the door to replacement.
