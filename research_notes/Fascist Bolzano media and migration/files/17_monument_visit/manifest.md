# Manifest: 17_monument_visit

Collected 14 November 2026. Three kinds of file are here: the author's own photographs of that morning (`photos/`), the pictures cut from them (`images/*_visit.jpg`), and study copies taken by the researchers and by hand (`images/`, one PDF beside this file). What each photograph shows and where it went is also in notes 17 §1. Downloads were made with curl under a generic client name from pages served publicly without a login; the Catalogo generale dei Beni Culturali was opened by hand in a browser. Rights are recorded as the source states them. Every image was opened and checked against its name.
