# Row 18.16: the German printing of Bährendt B 39 (24 November 2026)

- Found by the author on oldthing (a dealer's listing; only the image addresses were given): front https://img.oldthing.net/i/webp/9985/52/86/95/52869532_0_s.webp , back …_1_s.webp. Larger sizes (`_l`, `_xl`, none) answered 404 with a placeholder; the small ones (317 × 500 and 500 × 317) were converted to JPEG. Shown on the site beside the Mamming copy (inv. 9010, OMA 80007380) at the author's request of 24 November 2026.
- Seen in the image, front: the same photograph as the Mamming copy (the portal, the beam at top left, the steps, the plank floor); the caption "Meran, Romanisches Portal im Schloss Tirol" alone at the top. The scan is nearly monochrome, so the colour of the caption (red on the Mamming copy, under the bars) cannot be told.
- Seen in the image, back (crop `crop_imprint_big.jpg`): "Photographie und Verlag von Leo Bährendt, Meran. 1913. No. B. 39" printed upright at the left edge; the year read on a small scan. A five-figure number in the stamp box, unreadable (the Mamming copy has 280623).
- Reading: the Mamming copy is a card of this printing, overprinted on both sides: bars through the German caption and "Merano, Porta Romana" above it; "Fotografo e Editore Leo Baehrendt Merano" over the German imprint. "Romanisches Portal" (Romanesque) becomes "Porta Romana" (Roman) [INF from the two captions].
