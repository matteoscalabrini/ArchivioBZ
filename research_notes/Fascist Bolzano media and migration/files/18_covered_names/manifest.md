# Thread 18: covered names, downloads

Gathered on 24 November 2026 for `../../18_covered_names.md`. Proposed records are in `entries.draft.json` (not read by the site build). Scripts used a generic client name; nothing was taken from behind a login, a paywall or a technical block. Teßmann digital and the Catalogo generale were not touched.

**Sources and their rights.**

- **OMA** = the provincial portal Objekte Museen Archive (`objekte-museen-archive.provinz.bz.it`). Its record pages serve each image publicly through `Content/GetContent?command=getcontent&server=…&value=…&folderId=<record>&imageformat=jpg`; the copies here are those files as served (the portal's largest public size, mostly about 1000 px on the long side; four are only 300 px). Each record names its rights holder in a "Rechte" field, copied below word for word. No licence is stated: rights not stated beyond that; © presumed with the named holder. Study copies only.
- **Europeana** = the 2019 copy of the Teßmann newspapers and gazettes, served under the Public Domain Mark 1.0 (`http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/`, as stated in each IIIF manifest's attribution). Page images are the full IIIF files; the `_OCR.txt` beside each is Europeana's uncorrected full text of that page, with a one-line header.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: licence as on the file page.
