# Row 18.04: a second copy of Amonn M 10511 (24 November 2026)

## The second copy

- Found by the author on heimatsammlung.de, a collector's site: https://www.heimatsammlung.de/topo_unter/italien_ab_03/images-03/4893-postkarte-mendel.jpg (the folder page answered 403, so no description was read). Saved as `1920s_Amonn_M10511n_Strada_della_Mendola_Italian_reprint_front_back_heimatsammlung.jpg`, 841 × 1072, front above back in one picture, the site's watermark across the front; rights not stated. Shown on the site as the second picture of row 18.04, beside the Mamming copy, at the author's request of 24 November 2026.
- Seen in the image, front: the Mendola road cut into the rock face, the low wall, two poles and the car on the bend, the Adige valley and the mountains across it, as on the Mamming copy (inv. 25651, OMA 80079841). No title on the front. The sky is clear blue with small clouds; on the Mamming copy a bank of cloud fills it. The mountains stand larger and lit pink; on the Mamming copy they are a thin band under the cloud.
- Seen in the image, back (crops `crop_stamp_big.jpg`, `crop_foot_big.jpg`): "STRADA DELLA MENDOLA VERSO BOLZANO E LE DOLOMITI" printed upright along the dividing line, a green tree mark at its top; the oval "Joh. F. Amonn Bolzano"; "Riproduzione vietata."; "M 10511 n" at lower left. A violet rubber stamp "Hotel Caldaro / Propr. Herrnhofer / Mendola" (the proprietor's name as read); handwritten "8. 4. 30." (the last figure most likely a 0). No postage stamp, no address.

## Reading

- The same card number, M 10511 n, printed again by the same publisher with all its lettering in Italian, the place in the imprint now Bolzano, and the title moved to the back. The Mamming copy is the older German card corrected by hand: Italian title printed over the picture, German title under a black bar.
- The sky and the mountains were retouched for the reprint [INF from the two scans; which state is the photograph's is not known].
- My reading, not checked: the pair shows the two ways a card could become Italian, corrected or made new. The prefect's circular 6480 of October 1927 (draft 18.18) asked for exactly this on signs and labels, renewal rather than a visible strike; whether it reached postcards is not known.

## Open

- The Hotel Caldaro on the Mendola and its proprietor; the date of the reprint (before 8 April 1930).
