# Row 18.05: a second printing of Amonn M 14509 (24 November 2026)

Question: on the Palais Mamming copy (inv. 23354, OMA 80069713) the woman on the path has a pale halo and the wall's grain shows through her skirt. Was she painted in when the card was Italianised?

## The second copy

- Found by the author on Aukro (Czech auction site), listing "Merano (Meran) * město, hory, Südtirol, Alpy * Itálie * Z1409", listed 18 June 2025: https://aukro.cz/merano-meran-mesto-hory-sudtirol-alpy-italie-z1409-7017297764 . Seller's description: "pohlednice není prošlá poštou, bezvadný stav, formát 14 x 9 cm" (not posted, perfect condition).
- A dealer's photographs, rights not stated; the front is shown on the site as the first picture of row 18.05, beside the Mamming copy, at the author's request of 24 November 2026: `1904-25_Amonn_M14509_Kurort_Meran_in_Sued-Tirol_front_Aukro.jpeg` (front, 759 × 1200), `1904-25_Amonn_M14509_Kurort_Meran_in_Sued-Tirol_back_Aukro.jpeg` (back, 1600 × 1025).
- Seen in the image, front: the same view as the Mamming copy (pergola path, wall, St. Nikolaus tower and gable, mountains). Caption at lower left, German only, two lines: "Kurort Meran / in Süd-Tirol." No Italian text, no bar.
- Seen in the image, back: "Joh. F. Amonn Bozen" oval and "M 14509" at lower left; "Kunstverlag Leo Stainer, Innsbruck." printed along the foot; the "JFA BOZEN" mark in the stamp box; "5013724" printed upright at the right edge. Divided back, unused.

## Comparison (crops `crop_aukro_woman_big.jpg`, `crop_mamming_woman_big.jpg`)

- The woman is on the German printing, in the same place and pose, with the same blue bundle under her arm. She was not added by the Italianisation.
- A pale rim round her outline is visible on both copies, weaker on the Aukro scan. It belongs to the original printing, not to a later intervention [INF].
- The colours differ. Aukro: red band on the hat, yellow striped blouse, crimson skirt; the church roof red-brown, red trim on the tower. Mamming: pale hat and blouse, grey-brown skirt; roof grey-brown, tower without red. The red is weak or missing across the whole Mamming card, so the grain seen "through" her skirt is the dark printing showing where the red is absent, not paint laid over the wall. Fading of the red ink or a different print run would both explain it; the scans cannot tell which [INF].
- The Italianisation touched only the lettering: a black bar over "in Süd-Tirol." and "Merano. Chiesa parrocchiale" printed above "Kurort Meran", across her skirt.

## Correction

The reading given to the author earlier the same day ("the wall shows through her skirt: thin paint laid over a picture that already had the wall in it") was wrong; the second copy shows the figure printed in colour from the start.

## Open

- The Mamming record gives the series as "M 14509 n"; this copy reads "M 14509". Whether "n" marks another printing is not known.
- Leo Stainer, Innsbruck, on the back: distributor of this run; dates not checked.
- Whether the figure was photographed or added on the printing master by the publisher's retoucher cannot be told from scans at this size.
