## Deliberately NOT downloaded (URL recorded instead)

- **Bozner Messe 1921 poster**, attributed "O. Zoff, 1921". Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bozner_Messe_1921_Poster.jpg. Commons tags it PD-old, but the Palais Mamming record (https://objekte-museen-archive.provinz.bz.it/Details/bz/80031197) gives Otto Zoff's dates as 1890–1963, so in the EU it is still in copyright until 2033. Not downloaded.
- **Touriseum and Palais Mamming images** (all objekte-museen-archive.provinz.bz.it GetContent image URLs in 01_posters_graphics.md). Rights belong to the holding museum ("Rechte: Touriseum"), and many artists (Lenhart †1992, Puppo †1970, Romoli †1969) are still in copyright. Recorded by URL only.
- **Collezione Salce / Catalogo generale images.** The pages sit behind an "Anubis" proof-of-work anti-bot challenge, which was not bypassed. URLs recorded only.
- **Dealer images** (1stdibs, Antikbar, Galerie 1 2 3, Limitedruns). Commercial and in copyright. URLs recorded only.

## Metadata export (not an image)

- **oma_records_1919-1943.csv**: catalogue metadata (record URL, inventory no., type, title, date, makers, institution, description excerpt, and the search term that found it) for every poster, postcard, brochure, guide, map or programme dated 1919–1943 that came up in my keyword searches of the provincial portal https://objekte-museen-archive.provinz.bz.it (Touriseum, Palais Mamming, Amt für Film und Medien, etc.) on 2026-10-22. Search terms included Lenhart, ENIT, Puppo, Dudovich, Romoli, Borgoni, Dopolavoro, Siegesdenkmal, Monumento alla Vittoria, Fiera di Bolzano, Bozner Messe, Pedrotti, Funivia, Carezza, Littorio, Plakat Merano/Meran/Bolzano/Bozen/Dolomiti, Azienda Autonoma, Azienda di cura, Ponte Druso, Semirurali, Stadio, Schlern-Verlag, Traubenkur, stazione climatica, Unione Turistica Alberghiera, Brennero, Kurverwaltung Meran and others. Descriptions are truncated where the results list truncates them. Images are not included; rights rest with the holding institutions.
