# Manifest: 03_press_print downloads

Downloaded 2026-10-22 for the Studio1 "translocalities in/on Bolzano" project. Total size about 110 MB, under the 200 MB budget.

Rights summary:

- **Alpenzeitung (AZ) and Dolomiten (DOL) pages** are scans of Landesbibliothek Dr. F. Teßmann (Bolzano) copies, harvested through the Europeana IIIF service. Europeana labels each record with the Public Domain Mark 1.0 (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/). Every `_OCR.txt` file is Europeana's full-text resource for that page; it is raw and uncorrected OCR, so check quotations against the image.

- **La Provincia di Bolzano (LPB) front pages** are image-gallery scans from the Biblioteca Provinciale Italiana Claudia Augusta (webaleph.bpi.claudiaugusta.it); a stamp on several pages points to a Biblioteca Comunale di Trento copy. Under Italian law (l. 633/1941, art. 26-27) a collective, unsigned newspaper issue from 1928-1940 is out of copyright, since more than 70 years have passed since publication. The gallery itself states no terms of use, so treat these as study copies and ask the library before any publication. Reproducing them may also fall under the Codice dei beni culturali (art. 108).

- **The two Internet Archive books** are public domain in Italy because both authors died more than 70 years ago: Toniolo died in 1955 (public domain since 1 Jan 2026) and Borgese in 1952 (since 1 Jan 2023). Both were published before 1929, so they are also public domain in the USA.
