# Manifest: 07_browser_verifications

Screen captures taken by hand in Chrome on 2026-10-22 from platforms that refused scripted access (Teßmann digital runs a JavaScript browser check; the Catalogo generale dei Beni Culturali runs an Anubis proof-of-work check). Both cleared on their own in a normal browser; no CAPTCHA was involved. Captures are study copies of the viewer; the underlying newspaper pages are 1931 and 1933 issues of the Alpenzeitung, out of copyright, and are served by Teßmann with a "Downloaden / Drucken" button for proper downloads.
