# Manifest: files/15_propaganda_film_radio

Collected 12 November 2026 for the notes `../../15_propaganda_film_radio.md`. No video or audio stream was downloaded. A film is held as one poster frame or still that the holder's own page serves, plus the catalogue record saved as text in `records/`. Luce stills of newsreels are the poster frames of the films on the Archivio Luce Cinecittà YouTube channel, which the Luce catalogue page embeds and names as its own preview image (`og:image`); the larger `sddefault` frame (640×480) of the same video was taken where it exists. Luce stills of documentaries are the poster frames the catalogue page itself serves (`image.archivioluce.com/…/video_frames/high/…`, 320×240, with a burned-in timecode). Luce photographs are the 720-pixel images the photo record page serves, with the Luce watermark. None of the Luce pages states rights; the Luce material is © presumed Istituto Luce-Cinecittà, and these are study copies.
