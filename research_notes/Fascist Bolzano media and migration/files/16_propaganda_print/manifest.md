# Manifest: 16_propaganda_print

Downloaded 12 November 2026 with curl under a generic client name, from pages served publicly without a login. Nothing here was executed. Rights are recorded as the source states them; where the source states nothing, the line says so and whose copyright is presumed. Every image was opened and checked against its name.

Sources:

- **BPI** = Biblioteca Provinciale Italiana Claudia Augusta, Bolzano, open web gallery `webaleph.bpi.claudiaugusta.it`. The gallery states no licence. Its scans carry a large "BPI" watermark; several bear stamps of the Biblioteca Comunale di Trento, so the copies scanned are probably Trento's. The "large" file served is the largest version offered.
- **GU** = Gazzetta Ufficiale del Regno d'Italia, historical PDFs served by gazzettaufficiale.it. Official acts of the State are not protected by copyright in Italy (l. 633/1941, art. 5). Page images below were rendered by me from those PDFs (pdftoppm, 100–110 dpi); the PDFs are held whole.
- **Commons** = Wikimedia Commons. The licence was read from each file page (API extmetadata and page wikitext). The upload server refused full-size originals at times (HTTP 429, "use thumbnail images"); those files were taken as Commons' standard 960 or 1280 px thumbnails and are marked "scaled copy".
- **INDIRE** = FotoEdu, the online catalogue of the Archivio fotografico storico of INDIRE (Florence), `fotoedu.indire.it`. The record pages state no licence; the "original" download offered is 800 px wide and watermarked "INDIRE".
- **IA** = Internet Archive.
