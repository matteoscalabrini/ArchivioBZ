### Rights caveats
- `01_pontedruso__Drususbr_cke_1933.png` is tagged CC BY 4.0, "own work" by Bartleby08, but it shows a 1933 view. The real author and rights of the photograph are unclear, so verify before reuse.
- The Mario Gabinio photos (1932–35) are tagged CC BY 3.0 IT (source: Fondazione Torino Musei / GAM archive). Credit "Mario Gabinio / Fondazione Torino Musei".
- The 1960s postcard and the 1928 and 1944 newspaper pages are declared public domain on Commons, and the 1932 Eicke photo is PD as an anonymous work. Check the Italian and EU rules for anonymous photographs before publication.
- No openly licensed images were found for the Cinema Corso (Palazzo del Turismo, demolished 1988) or the Cinema Concordia (demolished 2018). See the notes, Q2 gaps, for where to look.
