## What this is

The research library of a Studio 1 project on Bolzano, made in the autumn of 2026. The studio's theme is translocalities in and on Bolzano. This project takes it through the fascist layer of the city: how Bolzano was made known to the rest of Italy and made Italian, in print, on screen and in stone, and what can be done with that material now.

Everything the research holds is here as a row: every scan, photograph, document, table and reference. Nothing was left out to make the list tidier.

## How it was gathered

The material comes from online archives, museum catalogues, newspaper libraries and statistical offices, gathered from 22 October 2026 onward. Each download is filed with a note of where it came from, when, and under what rights.

Two portals refuse automated access and were opened and read by hand: the newspaper archive of the Landesbibliothek Dr. Friedrich Teßmann and the Catalogo generale dei Beni Culturali.

The searching, filing and first reading were done with an AI research assistant. What it found was then checked against the sources, and each row says how far that checking went.

## How sure each row is

Every row carries one of three levels.

- **Read in the source.** The document, page or record was opened and read.
- **From a secondary source.** The claim comes from an article or book about the thing, not from the thing.
- **Lead, not yet a fact.** It comes from a search result, an index, a conversation or an inference. It is here so it can be followed, and should not be quoted as established.

Where sources disagree, the row says so and leaves the disagreement standing.

## Rights

Much of what is held belongs to its publishers and archives. The copies here are study copies, shown privately. Each row states the rights as its source states them. Nothing here is cleared for publication.

## The map

The map is Bolzano from Gries to the industrial zone as a field of dots, drawn from the Province's laser survey at half-metre resolution and from OpenStreetMap. Places that have entries in the library stand on it with their pictures, and their buildings are drawn at the survey's full resolution.
