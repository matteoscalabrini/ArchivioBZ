Fascism made Bolzano Italian in public: in the [[press]], on [[posters]], in [[newsreels|Luce newsreels]], in stone at the [[Monumento alla Vittoria]], the [[Casa del Fascio]] and [[Ponte Druso]]. How does such material lose its grip? Sometimes by [[removal]]. More often by a small change of form: another [[typeface]], another [[word|Wording]], another [[colour]], another [[scale]], another [[layout]], another [[voice]].
